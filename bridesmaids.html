Final Reminder

What to Bring

Pack these items before leaving for the hotel.

  • Bridesmaid dress, steamed and on a sturdy hanger. If you are unable to steam it beforehand, we will have a steamer available.
  • Required wedding shoes
  • Undergarments or shapewear
  • Minimal jewelry
  • Button-down shirt, zip-up top, or robe
  • Personal toiletries and overnight items
  • Phone charger and any required medication
Please do not wait until the wedding morning to check your items. Try on the complete look—including the dress, shoes, undergarments, jewelry, and hairstyle plan—before the wedding weekend.
