Pack these items before leaving for the Airbnb.

  • Bridesmaid dress, steamed and on a sturdy hanger, if unable to steam prior we will have a steamer present.

  • Undergarments or shapewear
  • Jewelry, minimal
  • Button-down shirt, zip-up top, or robe

  • Phone charger and any required medication
